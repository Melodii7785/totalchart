Methodology

TOTALCHART separates observed counts from estimates and weighted chart units. A raw stream count, a CD single purchase and a paid download are different kinds of activity; a simple sum is not automatically a fair popularity score.

  1. Keep streaming, physical sales and digital purchases visible as separate components.
  2. Show a combined total only when the underlying figures cover compatible products, territories and time periods and the calculation is explicitly defined.
  3. Mark missing information as “Unavailable” or “Not researched”, never as zero.
  4. Record the source, territory, date range, and whether each figure is reported or estimated.
  5. Do not count an album purchase as a separate purchase of every individual track unless the source's methodology explicitly allocates it that way.

Industry methodologies also use weighted equivalents rather than simply adding raw streams and sales. See Luminate's methodology and IFPI's 2026 methodology.

About TOTALCHART

TOTALCHART is an independent prototype exploring how music popularity can be represented across eras and formats.

Data coverage will vary by artist, release, country and period. A blank field means we do not currently have a usable source for that statistic.
