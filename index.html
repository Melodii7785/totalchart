    Methodology & data rules

    TOTALCHART keeps observed counts, estimates and weighted chart units separate. A raw stream count, a CD single purchase and a paid download are different kinds of activity; a simple sum is not automatically a fair popularity score.

    1. Keep streaming, physical sales and digital purchases visible as separate components.
    2. Show a combined total only when the underlying figures cover compatible products, territories and time periods and the calculation is explicitly defined.
    3. Mark missing information as “Unavailable” or “Not researched”, never as zero.
    4. Record the source, territory, date range, and whether each figure is reported or estimated.
    5. Do not count an album purchase as a separate purchase of every individual track unless the source's methodology explicitly allocates it that way.

    Reference material: Luminate methodology and IFPI 2026 methodology.

    About TOTALCHART

    TOTALCHART is an independent project exploring how music popularity can be represented across eras and formats. The catalogue is a work in progress; an empty statistic is not an invitation to make one up.

    Current version: searchable catalogue, artist filtering, sorting and individual song records. Data collection and source verification are ongoing.
