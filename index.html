How we count things (Methodology & data rules)

Spotify plays are not sales! We keep lifetime Spotify playcounts, physical sales, and paid digital purchases separate. Just because they're all numbers doesn't mean we can add them together. That wouldn't be fair!

  1. Streaming: TOTALCHART reads the playcount exposed by Spotify's Web Player Pathfinder. It is an undocumented source, is marked unofficial, and is captured with a timestamp.
  2. Physical sales: reported units must identify the product, source, territory, reporting period, and whether the number is reported or estimated.
  3. Digital purchases: paid downloads must be kept separate from streams and from album purchases. An album purchase is not automatically counted as a purchase of every track on it.
  4. Combined ranking: a cross-format score will only be calculated when comparable, sourced data exists for the selected entries and a transparent normalization method has been defined. Missing data is not zero.
  5. Coverage: country, period, product type, and source methodology must be visible. A worldwide lifetime count cannot be fairly compared with a one-week country-specific number without explaining the difference.

Right now, we haven't connected any physical or digital sales providers, so those numbers and the combined score are unavailable. We won't make up a number just to fill a space. That's not a chart!

Spotify lifetime playcounts are not an official sales or certification figure. A returned Spotify value of 0 may represent fewer than 1,000 plays; it is not proof of an exact zero.

About TotalChart

TotalChart is an independent little project for looking at music across different eras and formats. Search results and catalogue information are fetched when you ask for them. We don't have a giant list of songs sitting here already!

Search results come from Apple's iTunes music catalogue; Spotify links are used for the statistics pages. The artist overview's Top 10 list is included for reference, but it is not an official chart. Just so we're clear!
